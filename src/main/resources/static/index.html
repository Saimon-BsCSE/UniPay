FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Who can register and use UniPay? ▼

UniPay is restricted to authenticated members of United International University. This includes all active undergraduate and graduate students, faculty members, administrative staff, and authorized campus vendors/stalls operating on campus.

Are there any transaction fees for peer or canteen payments? ▼

No! All internal campus transactions are 100% free with ৳0.00 processing fees. Whether you're paying at the UIU Central Canteen or transferring money to a classmate for study materials, you pay exact amounts without hidden cuts.

How does SplitPay work for group canteen lunches? ▼

One student pays the counter bill using QR Scan. Afterward, that student opens SplitPay, creates a bill for the total amount, and selects friends. Friends immediately receive a live push alert with a 1-tap Accept button. When friends accept, funds are transferred straight into the creator's wallet.

How do campus vendors receive payments? ▼

Vendors have a dedicated Merchant POS Terminal. They can display either a static QR code on their counter or generate dynamic one-time QR codes for exact bills. Payments trigger instant audio sound chimes and increment their daily revenue statistics.

How does the simulated MFS Add Money work? ▼

UniPay features an integrated sandbox simulator for bKash, Nagad, and Rocket. In demo mode, enter your 11-digit mobile number and use the sandbox OTP 123456 to instantly credit your campus wallet.
